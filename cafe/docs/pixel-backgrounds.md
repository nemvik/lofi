# Pixel-art environments

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. The original cafe remains available.

Final project assets:
- `dist/assets/pixel-rain.png` — Pokoj v dešti.
- `dist/assets/pixel-roof.png` — Nad městem.
- `dist/assets/pixel-snow.png` — Chata v tichu.

All images are 1672 × 941 PNGs. Animations are rendered separately by
`dist/pixel-atmosphere.js` on a 480 × 270 pixel-aligned stage. Artwork is kept
still; rain, steam, snow, chimney smoke and stars animate at stepped intervals.

Final prompts:

## rain

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape pixel art background for a retro Czech lo-fi music player. Primary request: A cozy attic study at rainy blue hour. Large rectangular window centered in upper middle (x 28–76%, y 12–65%) with dark blue city buildings outside, amber desk lamp and wooden desk on right, steaming cream coffee cup around x 72% y 76%, vinyl records and potted plants on left, warm desk and an empty chair. Window has no rain streaks yet because rain will be animated by code. Style: authentic beautiful 16-bit pixel art, like a lovingly hand-drawn 1990s adventure game background, clearly visible square pixel clusters, sharp stair-step outlines, restrained dithering, consistent coarse pixel grid equivalent to 480x270 enlarged nearest-neighbor. No smooth gradients, no vector outlines, no 3D, no photorealism. Rich atmospheric lighting with limited harmonious colors. Composition: wide cinematic 16:9 edge-to-edge scene, dark quieter lower-left for UI title overlay, important content within center 60% to survive mobile crop. Nostalgic, cozy, detailed, peaceful and low-fi. No text, no letters, no symbols, no logos, no watermark, no UI or border. Output one landscape image only.
